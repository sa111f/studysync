About StudySyncer

Last updated: April 24, 2026

StudySyncer is a small, opinionated study tool built around one idea: the hardest part of studying isn't knowing what to study — it's actually sitting down and doing it. StudySyncer helps you do that through three tightly-linked features.

Pomodoro timer with real accountability

A classic Pomodoro timer, but with one twist: it emails you. Set a daily study goal in minutes, hit it, and you'll get a confirmation email the moment you cross the line. Miss it by midnight in your local time and you'll get a short, honest reminder. No streaks to babysit, no push notifications to ignore — just a paper trail of how you're actually spending your time.

AI study-time estimator

Paste your notes or drop a PDF of a syllabus. StudySyncer breaks it into topics and estimates how long each should take you, then helps you plan the work across your available time. It's a starting point, not a verdict — tune the numbers to what you know about yourself.

Tracker with deadlines

Every completed session is logged to a tracker you actually want to look at — focus hours, day streaks, material breakdowns, and a list of upcoming deadlines sorted by urgency. A "What to Study Next" widget on the dashboard tells you, at a glance, what to prioritise today.
